Draft only; not sent. Replace bracketed fields and obtain your school's/adviser's endorsement where required.

Subject: Request for anonymized station/route observations for waiting-time prediction research

Dear [agency or operator data officer],

I am conducting a Computer Science thesis on train and bus waiting-time prediction using Multiple Linear Regression and multi-factor station classification at [school]. I request available anonymized records for [routes/stations/directions] covering [start and end dates].

The preferred fields are:

1. Service date, station/stop, route/line, direction, and transport mode.
2. Actual vehicle/train-set arrival or departure timestamps and corresponding scheduled times, clearly distinguished. Please identify time zone and timestamp precision.
3. Observed passenger arrivals or counts by station/route/direction and time interval, with the counting definition and interval length.
4. Any passenger waiting-time survey records, including waiting-start and boarding times or measured elapsed waits, without passenger-identifying information.
5. Route distances and their definitions/units, and service interruption or unusual-event flags.
6. Data dictionary, known missing periods, collection method, and permitted research use/citation.

CSV or Excel is preferred. If the requested level of detail is unavailable, please identify what is available and its aggregation level. We will not represent schedules, modeled estimates, or daily totals as measured individual passenger waiting episodes.

The information will be used for [approved thesis purpose]. [Attach proposal and school/adviser documentation required by the provider.] Please advise on access procedures and applicable use conditions.
