# eFOI data requests (ready to submit)

Submit each request separately at https://www.foi.gov.ph (create an account, upload a valid ID, choose the agency, then fill in the fields below).

Before submitting:
- Replace every `[bracket]` (dates, stations). Ask for a recent, short period, for example 1 September to 31 October 2026; small requests are approved faster.
- Ask your adviser whether an endorsement letter from the college is needed; if so, attach it.
- Agencies normally reply within 15 working days, and this can be extended. Keep collecting your own field data in the meantime.
- Do not ask for the "six predictors". Each agency below is asked only for the records it is likely to hold.

LRT-1 is operated by Light Rail Manila Corporation (a private company, not covered by FOI). The request for LRT-1 and LRT-2 therefore goes to the Light Rail Transit Authority (LRTA), which owns both lines.

## How the replies are used

| Reply | Use in the thesis |
|---|---|
| Hourly passenger entries | Compare with or support your counted Passenger Volume |
| Actual train/bus trips per hour | Compare with or support your counted Vehicle Frequency |
| MMDA traffic records | Official basis for Traffic Condition |
| PAGASA hourly rainfall | Official basis for Weather Condition |
| Route length | Route Distance |

None of the agencies records how long each passenger waited, so Actual Waiting Time still comes from your own field timing. Mention the requests (and any denials) in Section 3.1.1; even a denial shows due diligence.
